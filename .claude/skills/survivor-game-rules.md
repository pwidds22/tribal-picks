# Survivor Game Rules & Show Mechanics

## CBS Survivor Episode Format
1. **Previously on** - Recap (no gameplay)
2. **Reward Challenge** - Winning tribe/player gets reward
3. **Immunity Challenge** - Winner(s) cannot be voted out
4. **Tribal Council** - Tribe votes to eliminate one player
5. **The Tribe Has Spoken** - Torch snuffed, player leaves

### Edge Cases
- **Double elimination** - Two tribals in one episode (both eliminations count for that episode)
- **Medical evacuation (medevac)** - Player removed for medical reasons (counts as elimination)
- **Quit** - Player voluntarily leaves (counts as elimination)
- **No tribal** - Recap/reunion episodes (no picks needed)
- **Idol play** - Negates votes; votes don't count, re-vote happens
- **Fire-making** - At final 4, loser is eliminated
- **Tribe swap** - Castaways change tribes mid-season
- **Merge** - Tribes combine into one (usually around episode 6-8)

## Game Type 1: Elimination Bracket (Season-Long)
- **When**: Before season premiere (locked at first episode air time)
- **What**: Predict the full elimination order of all castaways
- **Scoring**:
  - Exact position: 10 points
  - Off by 1: 7 points
  - Off by 2: 4 points
  - Off by 3+: 1 point
  - Correct winner: 25 bonus points
- **Lock**: Cannot modify after season starts

## Game Type 2: Cast Draft (Season-Long)
- **When**: Before season premiere (snake draft)
- **What**: Draft castaways to your team (exclusive — each castaway drafted once per league)
- **Draft format**: Snake draft (1-2-3...3-2-1...1-2-3)
- **Scoring** (your castaways earn you points all season):
  - Survive an episode: +5 pts
  - Win individual immunity: +15 pts
  - Win reward: +10 pts
  - Find a hidden idol: +20 pts
  - Successfully play an idol: +25 pts
  - Win fire-making challenge: +15 pts
  - Make the merge: +10 pts
  - Final Tribal Council (finalist): +20 pts
  - Win the season (Sole Survivor): +50 pts
- **Lock**: After draft completes, no trades or changes

## Game Type 3: Weekly Pick'em
- **When**: Each week before episode airs
- **What**: Pick who gets voted out + who wins immunity
- **Scoring**:
  - Correct vote-out prediction: 10 pts
  - Correct immunity winner: 10 pts
  - Correct vote split (bonus): 5 pts
- **Lock**: Episode air time (8pm ET on CBS)
- **Double elimination**: Must pick both if announced in advance; otherwise only first tribal counts

## Game Type 4: Survivor Pool (NFL Survivor Style)
- **When**: Each week before episode airs
- **What**: Pick ONE castaway who will SURVIVE the episode
- **Rules**:
  - If your castaway survives → you advance
  - If your castaway is eliminated → you're OUT of the pool
  - Cannot reuse a castaway (once picked, locked for the season)
  - "Survive the episode" = not eliminated during that episode (regardless of how many tribals)
- **Winning**: Last player standing wins
- **No points**: Binary in/out system
- **Lock**: Episode air time

## Pick Lock Times
- Default lock: Episode air time (Wednesday 8pm ET on CBS)
- Configurable by commissioner: 30min before, 1hr before, etc.
- After lock: picks are read-only, no modifications allowed
- Missed picks: In Pick'em = 0 points; In Survivor Pool = auto-eliminated
