About

Tribal Picks is the ultimate fantasy companion for CBS Survivor fans. Create leagues with friends, draft castaways, predict eliminations, and compete all season long.

Game Modes

  • Elimination Bracket — Predict the entire boot order before the season premiere. Earn escalating points for each correct prediction.
  • Survivor Pool — Pick one castaway to survive each episode. If your pick is eliminated, you're out. Last player standing wins.

Getting Started

  • Create an account with your email or Apple ID
  • Create a league and invite friends with your unique league code
  • Set your picks before episode lock time
  • Watch Survivor and check the app for live scoring updates

FAQ

When do picks lock?
Bracket picks lock at the season premiere. Survivor Pool picks lock at the start of each episode (8:00 PM ET on Wednesdays).

Can I change my bracket after the premiere?
No. Brackets are locked once the season begins. That's what makes it exciting!

What happens during a double elimination?
Both eliminations count. If your Survivor Pool pick is eliminated in any tribal council during the episode, you're out.

What about medevacs or quits?
These count as eliminations for scoring and pool purposes.
